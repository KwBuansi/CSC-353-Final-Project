How to Use

  1. Create a Google Form and paste the share link in the above box. Make sure to ask students to input their student IDs in the form.
  2. Enter a title for the QR code (e.g., "Quiz 1").
  3. Click "Create a new QR Code" to generate a code for the class.
  4. In the form's "Responses" section, click "Link to Sheets." Share the spreadsheet: Share → General access Anyone with the link as Viewer.
